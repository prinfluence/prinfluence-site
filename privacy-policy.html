Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

The short version: I collect your name and email when you sign up, your purchase details when you buy, and the work you save inside my tools. I use it to deliver what you asked for and to send you emails you can unsubscribe from anytime.

I don't sell your information. Ever.

This policy explains how Prinfluence ("Prinfluence," "I," "me" or "we") collects, uses and protects your information when you visit prinfluence.com, sign up for free resources, buy a product or use any Prinfluence tool. If you have questions, email talktome@prinfluence.com.

1. What I collect

Information you give me:

  • Name and email address when you sign up for a free class, toolkit, template or The Refresh newsletter.
  • Account details such as your email address and password when you create a login to access your products. Passwords are handled securely by my login provider and are never visible to me.
  • Purchase details such as your name, email, billing address and what you bought. Payments are processed by Stripe. I never see or store your full card number.
  • Work you save in my tools, such as brands in your Outreach Tracker, Content Vault bookmarks and Deal Worthy progress, so it's there on any device you log in from.
  • Text you submit to AI features, such as a pitch you paste into the AI Pitch Analyzer.
  • Messages you send me, including email replies.

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic technical data such as your browser type, device, pages visited and the site that referred you.
  • Information from cookies, pixels and similar tools (see section 4).

2. How I use it

  • To deliver what you signed up for or bought, including emails with your free resources and access to your products.
  • To send The Refresh and occasional emails about Prinfluence products. Every email has a way to unsubscribe.
  • To save your progress inside my tools and keep your account working.
  • To process payments, prevent fraud and handle refunds or support requests.
  • To understand how people use the site and improve it, including measuring which ads and links bring visitors.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

4. Cookies, pixels and browser storage

The site uses cookies and similar tools to keep you logged in, remember your progress and measure how the site and ads perform. Some come from third parties such as Google, Meta and YouTube, which may use them under their own privacy policies. Some tools also store information in your browser (local storage) so pages load the way you left them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features, such as staying logged in, may not work without them. You can also adjust ad settings in your Google and Meta accounts.

5. How long I keep it

I keep your information while you have an account, stay subscribed or for as long as I need it to provide what you asked for. Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. When information is no longer needed, I delete it or make it anonymous.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe from emails anytime using the link in any email, or by replying "Unsubscribe."
  • Access, correct or delete your information by emailing talktome@prinfluence.com. I'll respond within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws. I'll honor requests as those laws require, and I won't treat you differently for making one.

7. How I protect it

Your data is stored with established providers that use encryption and access controls, and each customer can only see their own saved work. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password for your account.

8. Children

Prinfluence is for adults building a creator business. It isn't directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given me information, email me and I'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If I update this policy, I'll change the effective date at the top. For significant changes, I'll let subscribers know by email.
