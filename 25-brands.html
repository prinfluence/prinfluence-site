Where paid briefs live

Most programs above pay commission or product. Flat-fee briefs mostly live on these platforms. Quote your Rate Floor.

Influee ↗

What it is: Paid UGC campaigns and gifting.

How you get booked: Apply to open campaigns.

Good to know: Rights and Spark Ads handled in the platform.

Billo ↗

What it is: Product video for ads.

How you get booked: Brands post briefs and book you.

Good to know: Brands may run it from your handle, so price whitelisting.

JoinBrands ↗

What it is: Paid collabs, sampling and seeding.

How you get booked: Apply or get invited.

Good to know: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Amazon. Video due in about ten days.

Insense ↗

What it is: Self-serve marketplace plus managed service.

How you get booked: Work through the app. Contracts and payments are automated.

Good to know: 100,000+ creators, so expect competition.

TikTok Creator Marketplace ↗

What it is: TikTok's discovery tool for brands.

How you get booked: Brands invite you. You must be 18+ in a supported country.

Good to know: Many projects start near 1,000 followers.

Instagram Creator Marketplace ↗

What it is: Meta's tool inside Business Suite.

How you get booked: Brands browse professional accounts.

Good to know: Less developed than TikTok's.

Source: Influee's 2026 guides on brands working with UGC creators. Programs open and close without notice, so confirm the page is live before you apply.
